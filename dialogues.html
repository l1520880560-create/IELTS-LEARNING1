No.6 Opening a Bank Account 开设银行账户 Conversation（对话填词）
Listen and write NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS for each answer.
听录音，每个答案填写不超过三个词。
【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Man: Good morning. I would like to open a bank account, please.
Woman: Certainly. Is it a current account or a deposit 1 you wanted?
Man: A current account, I think. What is the difference?
Woman: A current account is for everyday money, and you can use an ATM and a debit card.
Man: And what about the interest rate?
Woman: It is low, but you can check your bank statement online every month.
Man: Will this be a joint account?
Woman: No, just myself. How much do I need to open the account?
Man: The minimum sum is fifty pounds, and you will receive a card and a PIN within five working days.
Woman: Can I pay by credit card, or transfer money from my account in China?
Man: Yes, a money transfer is fine, and we also accept cash and traveller's cheques.
Woman: Do you offer a Master Card or a Visa Card with the account?
Man: Both are available, and you can choose your 2 card when you complete the form.
Woman: And is there an overdraft facility?
Man: Yes, but you must apply for it, and the bank will charge 3 if you use it.
Woman: I see. How often would you like to receive your statements?
Man: Monthly, please. And do you provide life insurance with the account?
Woman: We offer house insurance and travel insurance as optional services, but they cost extra.
Man: Fine. Can I open the account today, and how long will it take?
Woman: About twenty minutes, and you can withdraw cash from the counter immediately after you 4.
Let me answer a few more questions about the accounts. A current account is for everyday spending, and a deposit account is for saving money.
You can use the ATM to withdraw cash, and you will receive a bank statement every month.
If you and your partner open a joint account, both of you can use it, but each person needs a separate PIN.
You can pay by credit card, and most shops accept a Master Card or a Visa Card.
Please be careful not to overspend; if you do, the bank may allow an overdraft, but the interest rate is high.
For travel, a traveller's cheque is safer than cash, and you can exchange money at the bank or the airport.
To open an account, you need your passport and proof of address, and you must fill in an application form.
Finally, some account tips. You can pay by cash or by card, and the initial deposit can be small.
Your annual income and monthly consumption affect the interest rate, and a donation is not taxed.
A security code protects your account, and the bank sends a confirmation to your phone.
Before you travel, remember that a traveller's cheque can be replaced if it is lost, which makes it safer than cash.

让我再回答一些关于账户的问题。活期账户用于日常支出，储蓄账户用于存钱。
你可以用自动取款机取现金，每月会收到银行对账单。
如果你和伴侣开联合账户，双方都可以使用，但每个人需要单独的密码。
你可以用信用卡付款，大多数商店接受万事达卡或维萨卡。
请注意不要超支；如果超支，银行可能允许透支，但利率很高。
旅行时，旅行支票比现金更安全，你可以在银行或机场兑换货币。
开户需要护照和地址证明，并且必须填写申请表。
最后，一些账户提示。你可以用现金或卡支付，初始存款可以很小。
你的年收入和每月消费影响利率，捐款不征税。
安全代码保护你的账户，银行向你的手机发送确认信息。
旅行前，记住旅行支票如果丢失可以补发，这使它比现金更安全。

Interviewer: There are two shifts: a day 2 from eight to four, and a night shift from four to midnight.

Interviewer: The general manager, but your direct supervisor will train you in the first week.

Interviewer: You will answer enquiries at the front desk, and help with simple office work.

Interviewer: Yes, one month, and if you do well, you may get a promotion and a pay rise.

Interviewer: Yes, we provide basic insurance for all employees, and you can start next 4.
