No.11 Wildlife in Australia 澳大利亚的野生动物 Interview & Lecture（访谈讲座）
Listen. Decide if each statement is TRUE or FALSE, then answer the questions with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS.
听录音。判断每个陈述是正确还是错误，然后用不超过三个词回答问题。
【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Good morning, everyone. Today's lecture is about wildlife in Australia.
Australia is famous for its unusual animals. The kangaroo and the koala are the most well-known examples.
Kangaroos can jump very fast, and they use their strong tails for balance.
Koalas sleep for up to twenty hours a day, because their diet of leaves gives them little energy.
Not all Australian animals are mammals. The emu is a large bird that cannot fly, and the platypus lays eggs like a bird.
On the coast, you can often see dolphins playing near the beach, and sometimes whales pass by during their migration.
Unfortunately, many of these species are in danger. Pollution and climate change are the main threats.
Scientists believe that conservation areas can help protect these animals for the future.
Let me give you a few more examples from around the world. In Africa, the lion hunts with its family group, and the elephant is the largest land beast.
On the farm, we find cattle, sheep, goats and horses, and their young are called a calf, a lamb, a kid and a foal.
Poultry such as chickens are kept for their eggs, while a donkey or a pony may be used for carrying goods.
In the sea, the shark, the dolphin and the whale swim together, and shellfish grow along the rocky coast.
A bee collects honey from flowers, a butterfly lands on petals, and a dragonfly flies above the pond at night, like a small firefly.
Some animals are in danger: the polar bear, the wolf, the fox, the puma and the tiger all need protection.
A crocodile lives near rivers, and a tortoise carries its home on its back, while a frog is an amphibian.
Baby animals have special names: a bear has a cub, a dog has a puppy, and a bird has chicks.
Breeding animals on special farms helps keep the population strong, and breeding birds are protected during the season.
An ostrich cannot fly, but it can run very fast, and a falcon is famous for its speed in the sky.
Let me say a few more words about animal protection. A national park is a safe place for wild animals, and the government makes rules to protect them.
In the past, some animals were hunted for their fur, but now hunting is not allowed in most countries.
Scientists study animals in their natural environment, and a zoo helps people see rare species safely.
A pet needs care every day, and a vet checks its health and gives it medicine when it is ill.
Some animals live in groups and help each other, while others live alone and hunt at night.
Farm animals give us food and other useful things, and we must treat them well.
In winter, some animals sleep for a long time, and in summer they look for water and shade.
One more thought on animals. Every creature needs food and water, and insects are part of the natural world.
The feeding time at the zoo is printed on the gate, and marine life is studied in the aquarium.
Some animals have a special ability to smell or hear, and their behavior changes with the season.
【右栏 · 中文参考译文】
大家早上好。今天的讲座是关于澳大利亚的野生动物。
澳大利亚以独特的动物闻名。袋鼠和考拉是最著名的代表。
袋鼠能跳得非常快，它们用强壮的尾巴保持平衡。
考拉每天睡眠长达二十小时，因为它们的树叶饮食给它们提供很少能量。
并非所有澳大利亚动物都是哺乳动物。鸸鹋是一种不会飞的大鸟，鸭嘴兽像鸟一样产卵。
在海岸边，你经常可以看到海豚在海滩附近嬉戏，有时鲸鱼在迁徙途中经过。
不幸的是，这些物种中有许多正面临危险。污染和气候变化是主要威胁。
科学家认为，保护区可以帮助保护这些动物，造福未来。
让我再举几个世界各地的例子。在非洲，狮子与家族一起捕猎，大象是最大的陆地动物。
在农场里，我们有牛、羊、山羊和马，它们的幼崽分别叫 calf、lamb、kid 和 foal。
鸡等家禽是为了鸡蛋而饲养的，而驴或小马可能用来驮运货物。
在海里，鲨鱼、海豚和鲸鱼一起游动，贝类沿着岩石海岸生长。
蜜蜂从花朵上采蜜，蝴蝶落在花瓣上，蜻蜓夜间在池塘上方飞行，像一只小小的萤火虫。
有些动物濒临危险：北极熊、狼、狐狸、美洲狮和老虎都需要保护。
鳄鱼生活在河流附近，陆龟把家背在背上，而青蛙是两栖动物。
动物幼崽有特殊的名字：熊有 cub，狗有 puppy，鸟有 chicks。
在专门的养殖场繁殖动物有助于保持种群强壮，繁殖期的鸟类在季节内受到保护。
鸸鹋不会飞，但能跑得非常快，而猎鹰以空中速度闻名。
让我再说几句关于动物保护的话。国家公园是野生动物的安全之地，政府制定规则保护它们。
过去，有些动物因皮毛被捕猎，但现在大多数国家不允许捕猎。
科学家在自然环境中研究动物，动物园帮助人们安全地看到稀有物种。
宠物每天需要照顾，兽医检查它的健康，生病时给它药。
有些动物群体生活、互相帮助，而另一些独居并在夜间捕猎。
农场动物给我们食物和其他有用的东西，我们必须善待它们。
冬天有些动物长时间睡觉，夏天它们寻找水和阴凉。
关于动物的另一个想法。每个生物都需要食物和水，昆虫是自然界的一部分。
动物园的喂食时间印在大门上，海洋生物在水族馆中研究。
有些动物有特殊的嗅觉或听觉能力，它们的行为随季节变化。
参考答案：
技巧：讲座类先读题划关键词（balance / energy / migration）；听到 not all / unfortunately 等信号词要警觉。

【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Welcome to today's lecture on business management.
Many small firms fail in their first year, and the reason is usually the same: they have no clear plan.
A successful business needs enough capital to cover its costs, including rent, salaries and advertising.
Marketing is also important. You need to know your customers, so you should study the local market carefully.
Online shopping has changed everything. Many companies now sell their products through websites and social media.
Another key point is cash flow. You should check your bank statement every month and control your debt.
If a business offers a discount, it may attract new customers, but it must still make a profit.
Finally, good communication between employers and employees makes a team much stronger.
Let me finish with a few more business terms. In a corporation, the board of directors makes the big decisions, and the executives carry them out.
Senior managers report to the board, and the administration handles the daily paperwork.
A business organization needs money to grow, so it may raise a fund from investors, and investment in research brings new products.
If sales are poor, a company may put its goods on sales at a normal price or even below cost.
Marketing debt is dangerous: if a firm cannot pay, it may face a financial problem and finally bankruptcy.
Remember, a good business keeps a balance between profit and responsibility.
Let me finish with market notes. A commercial company plans its expansion carefully, and global trade connects every country.
International competition makes prices lower, and a new product launch brings attention.
The income from sales must cover the cost, and the value of the brand grows over time.
An advertisement increases sales, but the quality and purpose of the product still matter most.
A market invasion by a big company can change local prices, so small shops must be creative.
【右栏 · 中文参考译文】
欢迎参加今天的商业管理讲座。
许多小公司在第一年就失败了，原因通常是相同的：没有清晰的计划。

营销也很重要。你需要了解你的客户，所以应该仔细研究本地市场。

另一个关键点是现金流。你应该每月查看银行对账单，并控制债务。
如果企业提供折扣，可能会吸引新客户，但仍然必须盈利。
最后，雇主和雇员之间的良好沟通能使团队更强大。
让我用几个商业术语结束。在公司中，董事会做出重大决定，高管负责执行。
高级经理向董事会汇报，行政部门处理日常文书工作。

大公司对市场的侵入可能改变当地价格，所以小店必须创新。

No.13 Designing a Questionnaire 设计调查问卷 Interview & Lecture（访谈讲座）
Listen. Decide if each statement is TRUE or FALSE, then answer the questions with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS.
听录音。判断每个陈述是正确还是错误，然后用不超过三个词回答问题。
【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Good afternoon. In today's lecture, we are going to talk about how to design a questionnaire.
A questionnaire is a common tool for collecting data in research.
First, you should start with a clear hypothesis, or a question you want to answer.
Second, keep your questions short and simple, so that every respondent understands them.
It is also important to choose the right group of people to answer your survey.
Third, you should use both closed questions and open questions. Closed questions give you numbers, while open questions give you opinions.
After you collect the answers, you need to analyze the data carefully before you write your conclusion.
Remember, a good researcher always checks that the results are valid and that the sample is large enough.
Now let me talk about writing your research report. You need an introduction at the beginning and a conclusion at the end.
An abstract is a short summary of the whole report, and an acknowledgement thanks the people who helped you.
Use references and a bibliography to show the sources, and put the appendix at the back for extra material.
Format matters: use bold for headings, italics for book titles, underline for key words, and an index at the end.
Follow the word limit, add a coversheet with your name and course, and include the data analysis and the data collection method.
In your investigation, you may use a case study or an observation, and an interview gives you deeper interpretation.
Check that the results are not invalid, and proofreading is necessary before you print and submit.
If you are ill, you can ask for special leave, and the data collection may take longer than you planned.
A quotation from a book needs the page number, and the subtitle of a chapter helps the reader.
Your presentation should highlight the main points, and the outline on the first page guides the reader.
Now, a few notes on method. Evidence is the base of every conclusion, and a clear definition helps you avoid confusion.
An observation records what you see, and the investigation follows a plan.
Consider your limitations, and check that your data is relevant to the question.
Your concern about accuracy is healthy, and the criteria for success should be written down.
Data processing turns raw numbers into useful information, and a theory explains the pattern.
Your results should show the relationship between the two variables, and you must state the length of the study.
Data aids your conclusion, and a clear symbol in the table makes the numbers easy to read.
【右栏 · 中文参考译文】
下午好。在今天的讲座中，我们要谈谈如何设计调查问卷。
问卷是研究中收集数据的常用工具。
首先，你应该从明确的假设开始，或者一个你想要回答的问题。
第二，保持问题简短明了，让每位受访者都能理解。
选择正确的人群来回答你的调查也很重要。
第三，你应该同时使用封闭式问题和开放式问题。封闭式问题给你数据，开放式问题给你观点。
收集答案后，你需要仔细分析数据，然后再写结论。
记住，优秀的研究者总是检查结果是否有效，样本是否足够大。
现在让我谈谈撰写研究报告。开头需要引言，结尾需要结论。
摘要是对整个报告的简短总结，致谢感谢帮助你的人。
用参考文献和参考书目标注来源，把附录放在最后放额外材料。
格式很重要：标题用粗体，书名用斜体，关键词下划线，最后有索引。
遵守字数限制，添加带姓名和课程的封面页，并包括数据分析和数据收集方法。
在调查中，你可以使用案例研究或观察，访谈给你更深入的解释。
检查结果不是无效的，打印和提交前必须校对。
如果你生病了，可以申请特殊休假，数据收集可能比你计划的要长。
引用书中的引文需要页码，章节的副标题帮助读者。
你的演示应该突出要点，第一页的大纲引导读者。
现在，关于方法的一些说明。证据是每个结论的基础，清晰的定义帮助你避免混淆。
观察记录你所看到的，调查遵循计划。
考虑你的局限性，并检查你的数据与问题相关。
对准确性的担忧是健康的，成功的标准应该写下来。
数据处理把原始数字变成有用的信息，理论解释规律。
你的结果应该显示两个变量之间的关系，你必须说明研究的时间长度。
数据帮助你得出结论，表格中清晰的符号使数字易于阅读。
参考答案：
技巧：学术方法讲座注意 first / second / third 顺序信号词；hypothesis / data / results / conclusion 是主线。

【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Host: Welcome back to our programme. Today we have Dr. Green, an expert on urban development.
Host: Dr. Green, many people are moving from rural areas to the city centre. Why?
Green: Well, the main reason is jobs. Industry and tourism create more work in the city.
Host: But cities are getting very crowded, are not they?
Green: That is true. The population is growing fast, and we need more housing and better transport.
Host: What about the suburbs?
Green: Suburbs are growing too, but many workers still travel into the city every day, which creates heavy traffic.
Host: So what is the solution?
Green: We need better public transport, more green space, and cleaner factories.
Host: And what about the environment?
Green: We must reduce pollution and protect the countryside around our cities.
Host: Thank you, Dr. Green, for sharing your views.
Let me continue our talk about the city. The outskirt of the city is quieter, and the city centre is busy with shops and offices.
High rises stand in the business block, and the old town keeps its origin and landscape.
Long ago, craftsmen, carpenters, fishermen, hunters and merchants lived here, and forestry, fishing, manufacturing and mining were the main work.
The aboriginal people were the native people of the land, and the aborigines left rock art on the mountains.
Near the peak, the altitude is high and the air is thin, and the vegetation is different from the valley.
A square metre of land costs more in the centre, and a hectare or an acre is used on farms.
You can see an old fort, a castle and a canal, and the heritage site is intact and protected.
Please do not touch the ancient walls, and take shots only at a distance, to keep the sanctuary safe.
We measure the area in square metres and kilometres, and the peak season attracts many visitors.
One more view of the city. The construction of new buildings never stops, and the expansion of the airport brings more visitors.
A historical museum, a sculpture park and a painting gallery are open all year.
The international airport is east of the city, and domestic flights serve the whole country.
Politics and power are discussed in the government building, and the city council meets every week.
The city center is busy all day, and a fisherman sells his fresh catch at the market.
Visitors from Queensland and Canada enjoy the mild weather here, and every public building has a wheel chair access.

【左栏 · 英文原稿】
Hello everyone. Today I want to talk about healthy eating, especially for busy students.
Your body needs protein, vitamins and minerals every day. You can get protein from eggs, fish and lean meat.
Carbohydrates give you energy, so rice, bread and cereals are important, but do not eat too much sugar.
Too much fat and salt can lead to heart disease and high blood pressure.
Many students skip breakfast, which is a big mistake. A good breakfast helps your brain work better.
Drinking enough water is also essential, especially in summer when you lose water through sweat.
If you eat too much, you may become overweight, so watch your portion sizes.
Finally, do not forget to exercise. A short walk after dinner can help your digestion and keep you healthy.
Now let me answer some health questions. If you have a headache, toothache, backache or stomachache, you should see a doctor first.
A physician treats general illness, a surgeon performs an operation, and a dentist looks after your teeth.
For children, a pediatrician is the right choice, and a veterinarian treats animals.
Common problems include pneumonia, diabetes, insomnia, asthma and tuberculosis, and congestion makes it hard to breathe.
A sprain needs an ice pack and rest, and walking sticks help after an operation.
Medicine such as antibiotics, penicillin and aspirin is useful, but sleeping pills and painkillers must be taken with care.
The dose is important: an overdose is dangerous, so follow the instructions on the bottle.
In a clinic, you may visit the ward or the maternity section, and the emergency room deals with urgent cases.
Some people choose acupuncture as a therapy, and a therapist can suggest a remedy or a cure for your condition.
Remember that milk and fruit are good for you, and regular exercise keeps the heart strong.
Nutrition matters: calcium builds bones, and enough food prevents malnutrition, while too many calories make you obese or overweight.
Lack of sleep may affect your intelligence and eyesight, and some children have learning difficulty.
The Medicine department at the university trains physicians and surgeons for the future.
Let me end with daily health habits. An injury needs quick care, and mental health is as important as physical health.
Support from family and friends gives you motivation, and a positive attitude helps recovery.
Sleep is essential, and a regular routine reduces stress and anxiety.
Too much fat acid and cholesterol may block your blood vessels, and a kilocalorie is the energy unit on food labels.
An underweight child needs more food, and regular check-ups at local clinics help prevent problems.
After surgery, you need rest and a special diet, and the doctor will tell you what to eat.
An empty stomach may cause discomfort, and hearing aids help people with poor hearing.

大家好。今天我想谈谈健康饮食，特别是对忙碌的学生。
你的身体每天需要蛋白质、维生素和矿物质。你可以从鸡蛋、鱼和瘦肉中获取蛋白质。
碳水化合物给你能量，所以米饭、面包和谷物很重要，但不要吃太多糖。
