The Green Hunters: How Carnivorous Plants Feed

1

When Charles Darwin published Insectivorous Plants in 1875, the idea that a flower could devour a fly struck many readers as absurd. Plants, after all, were supposed to be passive and vegetarian: they stood still, absorbed water and sunlight, and converted carbon dioxide into sugar by photosynthesis. To propose that some of them had become hunters seemed to reverse the natural order. Yet Darwin, ever the patient observer, accumulated years of experiments showing that the Venus flytrap, the sundew and the pitcher plant not only captured insects but digested them for nourishment. His conclusion was unexpectedly simple: these plants lived where the soil could not support them, so they had learned to supplement their diet with meat.

1

1875 年，当查尔斯·达尔文出版《食虫植物》时，花会吞食苍蝇的想法让许多读者觉得荒谬。毕竟，植物应该是被动而"素食"的：它们静止不动，吸收水和阳光，通过光合作用把二氧化碳变成糖。提出其中一些植物变成了猎手，似乎颠倒了自然秩序。然而，达尔文这位耐心的观察者积累了好几年的实验，证明捕蝇草、茅膏菜和猪笼草不仅能捕捉昆虫，还会消化它们作为养分。他的结论出奇地简单：这些植物生长在土壤无法供养它们的地方，因此学会了用肉类补充饮食。

2

Most green plants never need to hunt because their roots extract minerals, above all nitrogen, from the soil. In a normal habitat, nitrogen is plentiful and plants compete for sunlight rather than food. But carnivorous plants occupy a very different world: the acid bogs, flooded swamps and bare rock surfaces where nitrogen is scarce almost to the point of deficiency. Here a plant that photosynthesises perfectly may still grow stunted because it lacks the one nutrient no amount of sunshine can supply. Over millions of years, evolution favoured any mutation that helped a plant obtain nitrogen from another source — and the most reliable source, in a world full of insects, was capturing the insects themselves. As a result, carnivory is not greed but adaptation: a compensation for a missing ingredient of life.

2

大多数绿色植物从不需要捕猎，因为它们的根能从土壤中提取矿物质，尤其是氮。在正常的栖息地里，氮是充足的，植物争夺的是阳光而非食物。但食虫植物占据着一个非常不同的世界：酸性沼泽、被水淹没的湿地和裸露的岩石表面，那里的氮稀缺到几乎成为缺陷。在这里，一株光合作用完美的植物仍可能生长矮小，因为它缺少那种任何阳光都无法供给的养分。经过数百万年，进化偏爱任何帮助植物从其他来源获取氮的突变——而在这个充满昆虫的世界里，最可靠的来源就是捕捉昆虫本身。换句话说，食虫不是贪婪而是适应：是对生命中缺失成分的一种补偿。

3

The best-known trap is the active spring trap of the Venus flytrap, native to the coastal plains of the Carolinas. Each leaf is hinged along its middle, and the two halves bear stiff bristles that interlock when the trap shuts. On the inner surface stand six sensitive hairs that act as triggers; when an insect disturbs two of them within twenty seconds, the leaf snaps shut in about a tenth of a second — one of the swiftest movements in the plant kingdom. However, the trap does not close for everything: raindrops and debris are ignored, because the plant measures the pattern of stimulation. Once shut, the leaf becomes a temporary stomach, secreting enzymes that dissolve the soft tissues of the prey while the bristles hold the victim fast. About ten days later, when only the empty shell of the insect remains, the trap reopens to await the next meal.

3

最有名的陷阱是捕蝇草的主动弹夹式陷阱，它原产于卡罗来纳的海岸平原。每片叶子沿中线铰合，两半边缘长有坚硬的刚毛，闭合时相互扣锁。内表面立着六根敏感毛，充当触发器；当昆虫在二十秒内触动其中两根时，叶子在约十分之一秒内啪地合拢——这是植物界最快的运动之一。陷阱并非对一切都关闭：雨滴和碎屑会被忽略，因为植物会衡量刺激的模式。一旦闭合，这片叶子就变成一个临时的胃，分泌酶溶解猎物的软组织，同时刚毛把受害者牢牢扣住。大约十天后，当昆虫只剩下空壳时，陷阱重新打开，等待下一餐。

4

Unlike the flytrap, pitcher plants take the opposite approach: instead of moving, they dig a pit. The leaves of genera such as Nepenthes and Sarracenia have evolved into deep, slippery tubes that fill with rain. A visiting insect is lured by bright colours and sweet nectar along the rim, but the inner wall is so smooth that a foothold vanishes beneath it. The victim slips into the pool at the base, where the plant's digestive fluid — sometimes aided by bacteria — slowly breaks the body down into nitrogen and other nutrients that the roots can absorb. In the tropics, the largest species grow enormous pitchers that can hold more than a litre of water, and some have formed a remarkable partnership with ants: the ants nest in the hollow tendrils and feed on the nectar, while their waste fertilises the plant. It is a mutual arrangement that benefits both sides.

4

猪笼草采取相反的策略：与其移动，不如挖坑。像猪笼草属和瓶子草属这样的植物，叶子已演化成又深又滑的管子，装满雨水。来访的昆虫被鲜艳的颜色和边缘的甜花蜜吸引，但内壁太过光滑，落脚点瞬间消失。猎物滑入底部的液体池，植物的消化液——有时借助细菌——把身体慢慢分解成根可以吸收的氮和其他养分。在热带，最大的种类长出巨大的瓶子，可容纳超过一升水；有些还和蚂蚁形成了非凡的伙伴关系：蚂蚁在空心的卷须里筑巢，以花蜜为食，它们的排泄物则给植物施肥。这是一种双方都受益的互利安排。

5

Between these two extremes lies a whole array of inventions. Sundews cover their leaves with glittering droplets of glue, each droplet a deceiving drop of "dew"; an insect that lands is stuck, and the tentacles then bend slowly inward to bring more glue and enzymes to the struggling prey. Bladderworts, which grow submerged in ponds, solve the problem differently: each tiny bladder carries a watertight door and a vacuum inside. When a water flea touches the trigger hairs on the door, the trap springs open, water rushes in and the prey is sucked inside in a few thousandths of a second. The principle is the same everywhere — detect the prey, confine it, then digest — but the mechanism could hardly be more diverse.

5

在这两个极端之间，是一整套发明。茅膏菜用闪闪发亮的胶滴覆盖叶子，每一滴都是骗人的"露珠"；落上去的昆虫被粘住，然后触毛慢慢向内弯曲，把更多的胶和酶带向挣扎的猎物。狸藻生长在池塘的水下，解决问题的方式不同：每个微小的囊泡都有一扇防水的小门，内部是真空。当水蚤触动门上的触发毛时，陷阱弹开，水涌进来，猎物在千分之几秒内被吸进去。到处是相同的原理——检测猎物、困住它、然后消化——但机制却再多样不过了。

6

{whether|Whether}} such plants actually prosper by eating meat was long disputed, and Darwin's own feeding experiments provided the decisive evidence. He weighed plants grown with insects against those denied them, and showed that the fed group produced more seed and grew taller. Later researchers confirmed the pattern: on nitrogen-poor soil, carnivorous plants absorb a measurable share of their nitrogen from prey, and some species obtain up to half their annual supply this way. This is not to say the plants are efficient in every aspect; trapping costs energy, and in bright sunlight a flytrap may find it cheaper to photosynthesise than to hunt. Therefore carnivory thrives only where the benefit outweighs the cost — a delicate balance that ecologists still study today.

6

这些植物是否真的靠吃肉而兴旺，曾长期存有争议，达尔文自己的喂养实验提供了决定性的证据。他把喂了昆虫的植物和得不到昆虫的植物分别称重，证明喂食的那组结出更多种子、长得更高。后来的研究者证实了这一模式：在缺氮的土壤上，食虫植物从猎物中吸收相当比例的氮，有些种类一年所需的一半都来自这种方式。这并非说植物在各方面都高效；设陷阱要消耗能量，在明亮的阳光下，捕蝇草也许会发现光合作用比捕猎更划算。因此，食虫只在收益大于成本的地方兴旺——这是生态学家至今仍在研究的微妙平衡。

7

Today these extraordinary plants face extraordinary dangers. The bogs and swamps they inhabit are among the most threatened ecosystems on Earth: drained for farming, dug for peat, or flood by dams. Many species are rare even in the wild, and their slow growth makes them vulnerable to collectors, who dig up entire plants to sell to nurseries. The international trade in carnivorous plants is now restricted by law, and conservationists rear endangered species in laboratories to restock wild sites. Yet protection is complicated by the plants' own secrecy: some species flower only once, others hide among mosses, and a few are known from a single valley. For researchers, every discovery raises a fresh question about how many green hunters remain undiscovered in the world's quiet wetlands.

7

如今，这些非凡的植物正面临非凡的危险。它们栖息的沼泽湿地是地球上受威胁最严重的生态系统之一：被排干作农田、挖泥炭，或被水坝淹没。许多种类即使在野外也稀有，生长缓慢使它们极易受到采集者的伤害，有人把整株植物挖走去卖给药圃。食虫植物的国际贸易现已被法律限制，保护主义者也在实验室里培育濒危种类以重新充实野外种群。但保护因植物自身的隐秘而变得复杂：有些种类只开一次花，另一些藏在苔藓中，还有少数只在一个山谷里被发现。对研究者来说，每一次发现都会带来新的问题：在这个世界安静的湿地里，还有多少绿色猎手未被发现。

8

The story of carnivorous plants offers a lesson that reaches far beyond botany. It shows how a seemingly fixed rule of nature — plants feed on sunlight — can be reversed when conditions demand it, and how small differences in a harsh environment multiply into striking differences in form. For the student of English, the same principle applies to vocabulary: a word is not a fixed thing but an adaptation to context, and the same root may generate dozens of related meanings. Whether we study a sundew or a sentence, the valuable habit is the same — to observe closely, to ask why, and to resist the easy assumption that what we already know is all there is.

8

食虫植物的故事提供了一个远远超出植物学的教训。它表明自然的一条看似固定的规则——植物靠阳光为食——如何在条件需要时被颠倒，以及严酷环境中的微小差异如何放大成形态上的惊人不同。对英语学习者来说，同样的原理适用于词汇：词不是固定不变的东西，而是对语境的适应，同一个词根可能衍生出几十种相关含义。无论我们研究茅膏菜还是句子，有价值的习惯都是一样的——仔细观察，追问为什么，并抵制那种"已知即全部"的轻率假设。
